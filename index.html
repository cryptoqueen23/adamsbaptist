Our Purpose

Rooted in the Great Commandment & the Great Commission

Adamsville Baptist exists to help people worship God, grow in His Word, and serve one another — through worship, ministry, teaching, evangelism, discipleship, and fellowship.

Meet Our Pastor

Nathan Feinberg

Pastor Nathan came to Central Texas after being stationed at Fort Hood and found a church home right here at Adamsville. He was officially installed as pastor on October 29, 2023, and serves alongside his wife Cheryl and their three daughters.

We can't wait to meet you.
